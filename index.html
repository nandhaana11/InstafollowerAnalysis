How to export your Instagram data

  1. 1
    Open Instagram → Settings → Accounts Centre → Your Information and Permissions.
  2. 2
    Click Export your information / Download your information.
  3. 3
    Select Some of your information, check Followers and following, then click Next.
  4. 4
    Select Format: HTML (or JSON) and Date range: All time.
  5. 5
    Extract the downloaded zip and open connections/followers_and_following/.
  6. 6
    Upload followers_1.html (or .json) and following.html (or .json) below!
